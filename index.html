Common questions

Frequently asked.

Wait — is the Free plan actually free, or am I going to get surprise-billed?

Actually free. Forever. You get 5 renders every month, no card required, no countdown timer, no "upgrade or lose your work" pressure. If 5/month covers your workflow, you stay free indefinitely. Pro ($19/mo) is for creators who need unlimited renders because they ship weekly or daily. The Pro and Business plans have 14-day trials — those trials do ask for a card because they include Pro features. The Free plan has nothing like that.

What's the difference between Free and "free trial"?

Huge difference. A free trial is paid Pro access for 14 days, then you're charged $19/mo unless you cancel. The Free plan is a permanent product tier — it has fewer features than Pro, but it never expires and it never charges you. You pick what you want up front. No card, no trial, no surprises.

Do I need a music subscription to use Loopline?

No — upload any audio you already own. Epidemic Sound, Artlist, AudioHero, your own recordings, CC0 content. Loopline is the mixer, not the source. Direct library integration coming to Business tier later this year.

Who owns the output?

You do, fully. On paid tiers every mix is 100% yours with full commercial rights. Source tracks keep their own licenses (whatever you had when you uploaded), but Loopline claims nothing on the output. Free tier outputs carry a small watermark — fine for drafts, not for publishing.

Will auto-ducking ruin my podcast sound?

Unlikely. Loopline uses voice-detected sidechain compression with adjustable attack and release — the same technique Hindenburg's "magic levels" uses, without you configuring thresholds. Preview every duck before rendering, override any segment manually.

What about Suno and Udio? Won't AI just replace this?

Eventually AI will generate long-form content well. But your existing stock library subscriptions aren't going anywhere — they're licensed, predictable, brand-consistent. Loopline mixes whatever source you prefer, including AI-generated stems. It's the production layer, not the generation layer.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, from your account settings, no email required. If you cancel mid-month, your Pro subscription continues until the end of the billing period, then downgrades to Free (you keep your account and all your mixes). No contract lock-ins.
